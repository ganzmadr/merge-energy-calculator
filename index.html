How the numbers work
2→1

Two items merge into one of the next level, so every level doubles the cost. A level 10 item takes 512 level 1 items.

x8

The power boost sets which level the producer makes: x1 makes level 1, x2 level 2, x4 level 3, x8 level 4, x16 level 5. Each tap costs as much energy as the boost. x2 unlocks at player level 20 and x4 at 40. x8 can only be switched on with 200 energy and x16 with 400, but once on they keep working as your energy drops, but energy refills by itself (1 every 2 minutes) only up to 100, so most of the time you'll use x1, x2 or x4.

+1

Some taps give a bonus item one or more levels higher. That's where you save energy. Bonus chances grow with the producer's level and with bigger boosts.

⚑

Items below the boost level only help in pairs. With x8, a single level 2 item on your board can't be used, so the calculator leaves it out.

Safe

Safe ignores rare lucky jumps of 3 or more levels. Average luck counts them, so it's cheaper but less certain.

Your test data

Tests make the energy numbers more accurate. Producer levels with a dot already have test data. Built-in tests come with the app; the ones you add are saved on this device.

Add a new test
  1. Clear some space on the board and pick a power boost.
  2. Tap the producer 20–30 times.
  3. Before merging anything, count the new items by level.
How many items of each level did you get?
